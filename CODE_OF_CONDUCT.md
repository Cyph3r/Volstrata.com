# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Everyone is welcome here. We want this repository to stay a place where a developer can
ask a basic question, report a broken example, or propose a change without worrying about
how they will be treated.

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be clear and concrete. "This example fails with X on Python 3.12" helps everyone.
- Respect that maintainers volunteer their time and may take a while to respond.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages — and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately through the contact link published on https://volstrata.com.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions
that do not meet this standard, and may block a contributor when the behaviour continues.
